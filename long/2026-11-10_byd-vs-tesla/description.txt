BYD sold about 276,000 more fully electric cars than Tesla between July and September 2026. How did a company that started out making phone batteries overtake the most famous car company on Earth? We go through the scoreboard, BYD's origin story and Warren Buffett's bet, its secret weapon (it makes almost everything itself, even the ships), Tesla's very different bet on robotaxis, the surprising profit numbers, BYD's problems at home in China, and why you can't buy a BYD in America.
