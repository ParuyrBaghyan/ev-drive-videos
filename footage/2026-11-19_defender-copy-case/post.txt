Why Land Rover is taking BYD to court over the Ti7 SUV.

Jaguar Land Rover says BYD's boxy Ti7 plug-in hybrid copies the Land Rover Defender, and in August 2026 court-appointed officers seized files from two BYD offices in the Netherlands; JLR reportedly suspects BYD took a Defender apart. In the UK the Ti7 is priced at £47,995, about £20,000 less than a seven-seat Defender 110 (£68,465). Learn the twist: on November 11, 2026 a Dutch judge ordered JLR to destroy or return the seized files, with fines of €50,000 an hour, because they weren't needed for the copying claim. That claim continues and no infringement has been found. Real copy, or just another boxy off-roader?
