DESCRIPTION:
Why plug-in cars hit a record share of China's car market.

In the first half of November 2026, electric and plug-in hybrid cars took 73.2% of China's new-car retail sales (CPCA), and the industry group expects more than 70% for the full month, a first. In the US, fully electric cars were about 6.4% of new sales in Q3 2026 (Cox Automotive). The twist: China's plug-in sales actually fell 13% from a year earlier; the share jumped because gas car sales fell even faster, with total car sales down 25%. Analysts blame high fuel prices, nervous buyers and smaller trade-in subsidies, and say the industry has entered a "survival" phase. The end of the gas car, or just a bad year?
