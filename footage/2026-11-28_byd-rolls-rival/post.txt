DESCRIPTION:
How BYD's electric Yangwang Altior limousine compares with a Rolls-Royce Ghost.

BYD, best known for cheap electric cars like the sub-$10,000 Seagull, showed its Yangwang Altior to China at the Guangzhou Auto Show (November 27, 2026, after a global debut in Abu Dhabi on November 7). It's a fully electric four-seat limousine, 5.6 m (about 18 ft) long with rear coach doors, four motors (one per wheel) and a China pre-sale price of 2.08 million yuan, about $311,000; a 2026 Rolls-Royce Ghost starts at $373,350 (KBB) with a gas V12. BYD claims 10-70% charge in about 5 minutes on its flash chargers. The catch: BYD hasn't disclosed power or battery size, and the 1,000 km (621 mi) range is on China's lenient CLTC test. Deliveries are expected from Q2 2027. Would you pay Rolls-Royce money for a BYD?
