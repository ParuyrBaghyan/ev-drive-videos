DESCRIPTION:
Why Tesla is worth so much more than carmakers that sell far more cars.

Toyota sold about 10.5 million vehicles in 2025, more than six times Tesla's 1.64 million, yet in November 2026 Tesla's market value (about $1.5 trillion) is roughly six times Toyota's (about $250 billion). Tesla's deliveries peaked at 1.81 million in 2023 and fell in 2024 and 2025, and in Q3 2026 its net income fell 43% from a year earlier ($1.37B to $787M). Learn what investors are really betting on: robotaxis (nearly 2 million unsupervised miles by the end of Q3 2026) and robots, with R&D spending up 69%. Skeptics say the payoff could take years; fans say cars were never the point. Car company or robot company?
