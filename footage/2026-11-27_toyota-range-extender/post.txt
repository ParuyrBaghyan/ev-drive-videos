DESCRIPTION:
What a range-extended electric car (EREV) is, and why Toyota is building one.

GAC Toyota, Toyota's joint venture in China, is revealing Toyota's first extended-range EV at the Guangzhou auto show on November 27, 2026. In an EREV, electric motors drive the wheels and a gas engine works as a generator, recharging the battery when it runs low. Learn why carmakers are rushing in: Xiaomi's first range extenders, the Sky Nomad series, launched in September and made up 27,147 of its 50,168 deliveries in October (54%), while Toyota's China sales fell about 19% in the first nine months of 2026. Nikkei reports Toyota is aiming for 200,000 EREVs in 2027. The catch: it still burns gas and is less efficient than a pure electric car. Smart bridge or step back?
