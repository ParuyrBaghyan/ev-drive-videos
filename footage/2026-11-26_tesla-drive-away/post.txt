DESCRIPTION:
How Tesla's Emergency Drive Away feature works, and what it costs.

With software update 2026.38.3 (October 2026), Tesla lets a car plugged into a US Supercharger drive off in an emergency: shift into Drive, hold the brake, tap Activate and accelerate, and the charging plug breaks away. Tesla added it after a shooting at a Supercharger station in Idaho. Learn the price: coolant spills from the severed cable, the car's charge port needs a mandatory service visit, Tesla says repairing the stall can cost up to $25,000, and deliberate misuse brings extra penalties. It doesn't work at third-party chargers or on pre-2021 Model S and X, and one driver in Austin has already triggered it by accident. Life-saving escape or expensive mistake?
